Living in a space where you feel like you’re moving forward, but you keep returning to the same situations, emotions, or patterns...  
This would be how I conclude my past year of living. 

However, the very thing I learnt from those memories of loss, anxiety and pain, was that the emotional dimension is probably the least interesting part of human experience.  
**The despair is just not interesting.**  

So today is not the first day of anything, except the rest of your life.
